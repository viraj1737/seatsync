How it works

1. Sign in

Use your college account to log in.

2. Pick a desk

Select a floor, date and time slot, then choose a free desk from the map.

3. Reserve and exit

See your booking in “My bookings” and tap the card at the exit gate to release it.

What you get

  • Live desk status for each floor and time slot
  • Two-hour reservations with clash protection
  • A simple admin screen for library staff
